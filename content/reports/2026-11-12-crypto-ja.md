Without a fresh catalyst of capital inflow—be it through ETF approvals or macro-driven liquidity injection—the pair is likely to remain range-bound on the downside, with any upside attempts swiftly met by profit-taking or hedging pressure. The fundamental bias, therefore, leans toward caution with a downside tilt.
