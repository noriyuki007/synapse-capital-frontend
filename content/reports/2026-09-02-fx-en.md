## 1. Market Environment & Fundamentals

The USD is receiving broad-based support as escalating geopolitical tensions between the United States and Iran trigger a classic risk-off reaction. Reports of confirmed U.S. strikes on Iran have pushed crude oil prices higher while dragging gold and equities lower, reinforcing demand for the dollar as a safe‑haven currency. This environment is boosting USD/JPY, which tends to appreciate when risk aversion rises and the greenback strengthens against the yen.

In addition to the shock factor, the dollar is benefiting from relatively firm U.S. yields amid expectations that the Federal Reserve will maintain a restrictive stance to combat inflationary pressures from higher energy prices. The yen, by contrast, remains under pressure as its traditional safe‑haven appeal is outweighed by the dollar’s superior yield advantage and the prevailing risk‑off sentiment. Consequently, the fundamental backdrop favors further upside in USD/JPY.
