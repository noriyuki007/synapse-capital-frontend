## 1. Market Environment & Fundamentals

The macro backdrop for USD/JPY remains defined by the tension between cyclical risk sentiment and rate-path uncertainty. The simultaneous reports of oil prices surging higher into the weekend and US fiscal 2026 posting the largest deficit in five years introduce two competing forces. Rising oil prices directly increase Japan's energy import bill, mechanically pressuring the yen through a cost-push inflation channel that typically favors counter-cyclical currencies. Conversely, a larger-than-expected fiscal deficit signals robust aggregate demand and potential upward pressure on real rates, which historically underpins dollar strength. The divergence between these drivers is the defining feature of the current environment.

The European-US tech divergence implies that global growth dispersion is widening, which complicates any broad-rate-correlation thesis.
