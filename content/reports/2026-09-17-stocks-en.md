The 10-year Treasury yield remained elevated, pushing the discount rate for equities higher and undermining valuation models across the market. This macro backdrop has triggered a broad-based sell-off, with the Dow falling 500 points and the S&P 500 experiencing sustained downward pressure as gains were systematically shaved off during the trading session.

Fundamentally, the market is grappling with the reality that tighter monetary conditions are here to stay, and the Fed's commitment to fighting inflation overrides short-term technical support levels. The mixed performance of US equity indexes post-rate hike highlights a lack of conviction, with selling pressure accelerating as institutional players adjust their portfolios to account for higher borrowing costs. In this environment, equity indexes face a structural headwind, and the path of least resistance for the S&P 500 remains to the downside until clear signs of yield stabilization and a dovish policy shift emerge.
