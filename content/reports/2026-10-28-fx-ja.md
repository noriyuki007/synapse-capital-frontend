## 1. 市場環境とファンダメンタルズ分析

The macro environment is characterized by a hawkish shift in G10 monetary policy. The Bank of Canada raised its policy rate by 25 bp to 2.50 % on 2026‑10‑28, with market pricing now reflecting a 78 % probability of further tightening. This move has lifted the CAD yield curve, prompting a modest retracement in USDCAD, but the underlying US dollar remains resilient on the back of elevated US real yields. In parallel, rising crude oil prices are exerting downward pressure on the Indian rupee despite repeated RBI interventions, reinforcing the broader risk‑off bid for the US dollar.

For USD/JPY, the combination of higher US yields and a still‑accommodative Bank of Japan creates a favorable carry environment. The JPY continues to function as a funding currency, and any incremental hawkish repricing in US rate expectations tends to amplify the pair’s upward momentum. Absent a sudden BoJ policy surprise, the fundamental backdrop supports a gradual appreciation of USD/JPY.
