## 1. Market Environment & Fundamentals

The global bond selloff, with yields surging across major sovereign curves, introduces additional complexity. While higher US yields would traditionally support the dollar leg of the pair, the simultaneous repricing of Japanese yield expectations and the Bank of Japan's policy normalization trajectory are narrowing the rate differential that has historically underpinned USD/JPY. This convergence of factors favors continued yen appreciation in the near term, particularly as geopolitical risk premiums remain elevated.
